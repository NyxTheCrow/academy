# 06 · Open Questions

The running list of things not yet settled. Each has the options and the current
lean, so future-you doesn't re-litigate from scratch.

1. **Setting A vs B first** → **A (cozy/political)**, provisional. B stays a
   later mode/campaign/endgame. (`04`)

2. **Mana vs Aura vs both** → **[OPEN]**. Caster-flavored "mana" vs
   warrior-flavored "aura"; possibly two distinct pools. Decide when casting
   rules are prototyped. (`02`)

3. **How many resource stats is too many** → **[OPEN]**. Six are on the table
   (fatigue, bandwidth, mana/aura, sanity, morale, injury). Lean: **start with a
   small subset**; add the rest with the systems that need them. (`02`)

4. **Is numberlessness + dev mode enough for optimizers** → **[OPEN]**. Lean:
   add **divination** as the in-fiction "aim at outcomes" tool so optimizing is
   gameplay, not a fight with the UI. (`01`)

5. **Randomness amplitude curve** → **[OPEN]**. Principle decided (variance ∝
   inverse skill, bounded); actual numbers TBD after a casting prototype. (`03`)

6. **Separate physical vs mental fatigue** → leaning **yes** (they model
   genuinely different pressures), but it's the kind of split that can bloat.
   Validate it earns its place in a slice. (`02`)

7. **How present is sanity/dark magic in the cozy base** → lean **latent**;
   blooms along darker paths / in campaign B. (`02`, `04`)

8. **The social sim** → acknowledged as **the hardest part** and the biggest
   open design risk. No solution yet; it's where the most thought is owed.

9. **Scope of the first vertical slice** → **[OPEN]**. What is the *smallest*
   slice that already has **charm** (not just working systems)? Probably: one
   week of school, a couple of teachers who *comment* on your work, one social
   thread, one graded skill shown purely through feedback.

10. **Making it for yourself vs an audience** → **for yourself** — but note the
    tension: some of the above (feedback clarity) is where "for an audience"
    pulls back the other way. Hold it consciously.
