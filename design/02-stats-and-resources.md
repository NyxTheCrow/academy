# 02 · Stats & Resources

Working model of the resource stats. All are **hidden numbers surfaced as
sensations/words**. The through-line: resources create *pressure* that forces
variety and prevents spam. Several are distinct on purpose.

## The resources (working draft)

| Resource | Timescale | Restored by | Purpose / feel |
|----------|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Physical Fatigue** | Day-to-day | Sleep | The body's tiredness. Gates physical action. ("energy" in the build.) |
| **Mental Fatigue / Bandwidth** | Longer, creeping | Rest, leisure, variety | Burnout. Caps how much you can *learn* per day — the anti-study-spam valve. Sims-"fun"-like. |
| **Mana** (and/or **Aura**) | Per-scene / recharges | Rest, focus | Fuel for casting. |
| **Sanity** | Long, sticky | Hard to recover | Erodes with dark magic / horror. Low → consequences. |
| **Morale / Mood** | Medium | Good days, bonds | Emotional wellbeing. (Present in the build as the one qualitative stat.) |
| **Injury** | Long | Healing, time | Lasting physical harm from combat / dungeoneering (≠ daily fatigue). |

## Why physical ≠ mental fatigue [DECIDED: keep them separate]

Being tired from *running* is not the same as being tired from *studying*.
Daily physical fatigue is the sleep loop; **mental fatigue is burnout** — the
ceiling on continuous studying. Cramming all day should hit diminishing returns
and force you to do something else. Both are spent against the shared budget of
**time** (already modeled), so they interact with scheduling naturally.

- Framing that landed: mental fatigue is closer to a **"fun"/wellbeing** stat
  than to sleepiness. Continuous study → burnout → you *need* relief.

## Mana vs Aura [OPEN]

- "Mana" reads **caster**; "Aura" reads **warrior / manhwa-manga**. They could
  coexist as *distinct pools* (e.g., mana = spell fuel; aura = a bodily/martial
  charge), or one name could cover the magical-energy concept. Undecided.

## Sanity & dark magic [DECIDED there will be sanity mechanics]

- Dark magic exists in the world; **sanity** tracks exposure to horror/dark
  practice. Low sanity has teeth. In the darker campaign (see `04`), it can go
  as far as **things spawning** when it bottoms out. In the cozy base it can sit
  **latent**, blooming only along darker paths.

## Injury [LATER]

Long-term physical consequence, distinct from the daily fatigue loop. Naturally
tied to dungeoneering, dueling, and missions.

## Risk to watch [OPEN]

That's potentially **six** resource channels (fatigue, bandwidth, mana/aura,
sanity, morale, injury). Real question for the first slice: **how many can
carry their weight** before it becomes the very spreadsheet we're avoiding?
Likely start with a **small subset** (physical fatigue + mental/bandwidth +
one magical resource + morale) and introduce sanity/injury with the systems
that need them.
