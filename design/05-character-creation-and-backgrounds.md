# 05 · Character Creation & Backgrounds

## Backgrounds are the big multiplier [DECIDED]

Character creation and **selectable backgrounds decide a LOT** — they're the
biggest branch multiplier in the game. **Many checks and events key off them.**
Treat character creation as a first-class system, not a menu.

A background should be able to set:
- **Starting skills/tags** (the per-thing booleans from `03`).
- **Starting relationships & reputation** (who knows you, who resents you).
- **Which action and event branches open or close** wholesale.
- **The character's stakes** — the lens the whole campaign is read through.

## Social position is *dynamic*, not just a start state [DECIDED]

Class isn't only chosen at creation — it's a **campaign axis** you move along
through play. You can drift:
- **Toward highborn** — buy/forge respectability, lean into a questionable
  descent; or
- **Toward the lowborn / union side** — throw in with the commoners.

The interesting space is being **caught between the classes** — able to tip
either way. This mobility is decided by **what you do during the campaign**, not
just the background you picked.

## Marquee example: fake descent

The **passing-as-noble** background (from `04`) is the showcase: it seeds a long
**discovery-risk arc**. Creation sets the lie; the campaign is the tension of
maintaining or escaping it.

## Design implication

Because so much branches on backgrounds, content should be authored as
**condition-gated fragments** keyed to background tags/flags — write once,
gate many. This keeps the branching authorable by one person.
