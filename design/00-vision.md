# 00 · Vision

## Elevator pitch

An **incremental, UI-based, minute-to-minute time-management life sim** about a
student at a magic academy — with **VN elements**, **tactical combat**, and
(later) **dungeoneering / dueling / missions**. Text- and UI-driven; **no
roaming map**. Built by one person, so scope is chosen accordingly.

## Design pillars

1. **Charm over legibility.** The whole project is a reaction against the
   "loveless, charmless spreadsheet simulator" feel. A character should read as
   a *person*, not a stat block. If a choice trades away optimizer-legibility
   for life and texture, that's usually the right trade.
2. **Numberless on the surface.** The sim runs on numbers underneath, but the
   player sees **words, grades, sensations** — diegetic feedback, not a stat
   readout. (A dev mode exposes the numbers.)
3. **Information is the reward.** Skills mainly unlock **perception and
   understanding**, not just success rolls. Getting better means the world
   *tells you more* — the Disco Elysium loop.
4. **A living character.** Feedback is teacher comments, bodily sensation,
   social read — the character has an inner life the UI reflects.
5. **Scope realism.** One dev, no 2D/3D map, UI + text only. Build the **cozy
   base first**; layer danger and darker systems later.

## North stars

- **Disco Elysium** (and the Disco-like **Esoteric Ebb**) — the gold standard
  for a textual feedback loop where hidden checks surface *information*.
- **The Life and Suffering of Sir Brante** — resource-gated choices (its
  "stamina" stat forces failure choices) and the idea of aiming toward visible
  **fates** (reframe-able here as *divination*).
- **The Sims** — a "fun"/mood axis as a pressure that forces variety.
- **Magical princess / tamagotchi-style sims** — the nurture loop, at a glance.

## Anti-stars (what to avoid)

- **Academagia** — the explicit anti-model: "a loveless, charmless spreadsheet
  simulator," "an XCEL simulator," "no life." Its core loop is numbers-go-up,
  and that's the charmlessness to escape.
- **Kings of Dragon Pass** — admired, but left a similar "never finished it /
  lifeless" taste; a caution about opacity without warmth.

## Format decisions [DECIDED]

- No exploration map (2D/3D). Movement is menu/room selection.
- Minute-to-minute time granularity (finer than Brante's chapter-scale life).
- Player-facing surface is numberless; a dev/transparency mode shows numbers.
